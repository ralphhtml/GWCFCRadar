The short version

GWCFC Radar is a weather map run by one person in their spare time. It is free, there is nothing to buy, and none of your data is sold or used for advertising. There are no analytics or tracking scripts on the page.

Most of what the app remembers about you never leaves your device. If you do not sign in, do not turn on location, and do not post a photo or a chat message, then nothing about you is stored on any server at all.

The one thing to read even if you read nothing else: this app is not an official warning service. It does not replace NOAA Weather Radio, Wireless Emergency Alerts, or what your local emergency management tells you to do. See Safety.

What is stored, and where

Two different places, and the difference matters.

On your device only

Kept in your browser's own storage. It never reaches a server, and clearing your browser data deletes all of it:

  • Every setting: units, colours, map style, layer order, opacity, themes, keyboard bindings, which panels are open.
  • Your starred places and features, and your saved model comparisons.
  • Inspector pins, drawings, storm cones, and text you place on the map.
  • Weather radio recordings you make, and the videos you attach to a Cloud Capture post (only the still thumbnail is uploaded, up to 60 MB stays here).
  • Navigation reports you file, which are also deleted automatically after four hours.

On a server, only if you choose to

There is no analytics package, no advertising identifier, no session recording, and no third-party tracking pixel anywhere in this app.

Location

The app asks for your location in three situations, and never otherwise:

  • Locate. One reading, used to centre the map, then forgotten.
  • Location tracking and navigation. A continuous reading while it is switched on, used to draw where you are, work out a route, and colour that route by the weather along it. It stays in the browser.
  • Rain Near Me and alert notifications. Your position is compared against radar and against active warnings, in the browser, to decide whether to notify you.

Your position is not uploaded, logged, or shared, with one exception you choose explicitly: the location attached to a Cloud Capture post or a navigation report, because a photo of a wall cloud with no location on it is of no use to anybody.

Routing is calculated by an external routing service, which necessarily receives the start and end points of the route you ask for. Turn location off in your browser or device settings at any time and the rest of the app keeps working.

Photos, chat and anything else you post

Cloud Capture posts and chat messages are public to everyone using the app. Treat them that way. Do not post anything you would not want a stranger to see, and think before you post a photo taken at your own house.

Photos submitted to Cloud Capture pass through an automated check before they appear on the map. That check is not perfect. Anything that gets through and should not have can be reported through Feedback and will be removed.

Videos are different from photos on purpose: the clip stays on the device that submitted it and only its first frame is uploaded, so a video is playable where it was made and shows as a thumbnail everywhere else.

Who else your browser talks to

A weather map is mostly other people's data. Your browser fetches directly from these, which means each one can see your IP address and what you asked for, the same as visiting any website:

  • NOAA and the National Weather Service, including SPC, WPC, NHC, CPC and the NEXRAD and MRMS archives, for radar, alerts, outlooks and forecasts.
  • Open-Meteo for forecast, marine and air quality fields.
  • Google Firebase for accounts, chat and Cloud Capture.
  • Google Gemini, if you ask Asturio AI a question. Your question and the on-screen weather context are sent so it can answer.
  • MapTiler and OpenStreetMap for the base map tiles.
  • Google Fonts for the typeface.
  • Blitzortung for lightning, SpotterNetwork for spotters and chasers, Ambient Weather for personal weather stations, Environment Canada for Canadian alerts, and state transport agencies for traffic cameras.
  • Public CORS relays, for a handful of agency endpoints that refuse to answer a browser directly. A relay sees the request and your IP; nothing personal is put in one.
  • A Raspberry Pi run by the developer, which renders the model charts, satellite composites and soundings.

Each of these has its own privacy policy and none of them is controlled by this app. Every one is listed with a link in Credits, inside the app.

Your control over any of it

  • Everything on your device goes when you clear your browser's site data, or through the reset controls in Settings.
  • Your account and everything attached to it can be deleted on request. Ask through Feedback and it will be removed, along with your chat messages and Cloud Capture posts.
  • Individual posts can be removed on request without deleting your account.
  • Never signing in at all is a supported way to use this app. Nearly every feature works signed out.

This app is not aimed at children under 13 and accounts should not be created for them.

Terms of use

1. What this is

GWCFC Radar is provided free, as is, with no warranty of any kind. It is a situational awareness tool built by a hobbyist, not a commercial product and not an official source.

2. No warranty, and what that means in practice

Weather data can be late, wrong, incomplete or missing. Radar sites go down. Models are wrong routinely, and that is normal. Feeds change shape without notice. Nothing here is guaranteed to be accurate, current or available, and the developer is not liable for any loss, injury or damage arising from use of this app or reliance on anything it shows.

3. Data you post

You keep ownership of photos, videos and messages you submit. By posting them you grant permission to display them in the app to other users. Do not post anything unlawful, harassing, or that you do not hold the rights to, and do not post other people's private information. Anything breaking that will be removed, and accounts may be suspended.

4. Fair use of the data feeds

The public agencies and free APIs behind this app pay for their own bandwidth. Do not scrape the app, automate requests through it, or use it in a way that would put load on those services beyond ordinary personal use.

5. Third-party terms come with the data

Some sources carry their own conditions, and using that data through this app means agreeing to them too. The machine-learning cyclone models are research products with their own terms of use, and are experimental guidance rather than a forecast.

6. Broadcast and re-use

StormStream mode and the export tools exist so this app can be used on stream and on air, and that is welcome. Credit the underlying agencies, and do not present GWCFC products as official National Weather Service products. They are tagged distinctly in the app for exactly that reason.

7. Changes

These terms and the privacy notice above can change as the app does. The date at the top of this page says when they last did, and material changes are announced in the update bar above the logo.

Safety

This app does not replace official warnings. During dangerous weather, act on NOAA Weather Radio, Wireless Emergency Alerts, and your local emergency management, in that order, before anything you see here.

The weather radio streams in this app come from third-party relays and can drop or be region-blocked. They are a convenience, not a receiver.

Navigation is for planning a route around weather before you set off, and for a passenger to watch. It is not a reason to look at a radar app while driving.

Contact

Anything about this page, a data removal request, or a problem with something somebody posted: use the Feedback button in the app, under the logo. It reaches the developer directly and needs no account.
